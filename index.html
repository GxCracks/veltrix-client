WHY VELTRIX?

Built for people who actually switch versions.

Keep your Minecraft versions, Fabric setups and client files organized without turning the launcher into a mess. VELTRIX is designed to feel practical, fast and easy to live with every day.

From old PvP versions to current Fabric profiles, everything stays easier to manage when each setup has its own place.

Separate installations

Keep 1.8.9, 1.21, Fabric test builds and future client setups in their own folders. Mods, settings and resource files stay separated instead of colliding in one installation.

VersionsModsSettingsResourcepacks

Version switching

Jump between older and newer Minecraft versions without rebuilding your setup every time you want to play something else.

Fabric-ready profiles

Create clean Fabric-based profiles and keep the client layer organized without burying everything inside the official launcher layout.

Cleaner performance control

Java selection, launch feedback and separated files make it easier to troubleshoot problems and keep the game running the way you expect.

Focused interface

A clean dark UI, readable spacing and fewer distractions help the launcher feel like a real tool instead of a generic template.

Ready to grow

Profiles, news, add-ons and future launcher systems can expand over time without forcing a full redesign each time something new is added.
